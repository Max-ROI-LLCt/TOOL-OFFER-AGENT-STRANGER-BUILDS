THE TRUST BOUNDARY

What it does not claim.

An attributed source tells you where a statement came from. It does not prove the seller's service behaves as advertised.

Tool Offer Agent does not independently certify sellers, authenticate external provider capabilities, automatically publish offers or spend money during drafting.

FAHCE is a separate Kingan Logic integrity service. Its wallet-approved x402 checkout purchases one Diagnose evaluation of the exact submitted offer and supplied evidence. The result can be negative or require repair.

Wallet authorization, service delivery, gateway-reported settlement and receipt proof are separate evidence. This does not claim native Fetch payment settlement, arbitrary merchant support, or execution of the API described in your draft.

Preparation is not permission to spend. A receipt is not seller certification.
